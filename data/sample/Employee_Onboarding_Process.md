# Employee Onboarding Process

## First Week
New employees receive system access, device setup, security training, and a mentor assignment during the first week. Managers must schedule a role expectations meeting by day 3.

## Required Training
All employees must complete security awareness, privacy basics, code of conduct, and data classification training within 14 days of joining.

## Access Requests
Access requests must be submitted through the identity portal. Privileged access requires manager approval and security review. Temporary elevated access expires automatically after 7 days unless renewed.
