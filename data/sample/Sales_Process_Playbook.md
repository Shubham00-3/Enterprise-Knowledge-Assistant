# Sales Process Playbook

## Opportunity Stages
Opportunities move through discovery, qualified, solution validation, commercial review, legal review, and closed won or closed lost. Stage exit criteria must be documented in the CRM.

## Discount Approval
Discounts up to 10 percent can be approved by the account executive. Discounts from 11 to 25 percent require sales manager approval. Discounts above 25 percent require VP approval.

## Handoff To Customer Success
Closed won opportunities must include implementation goals, success criteria, stakeholder map, technical constraints, and promised timelines before customer success accepts handoff.
