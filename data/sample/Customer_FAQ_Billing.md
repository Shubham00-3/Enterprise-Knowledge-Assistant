# Customer FAQ Billing

## Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase if the account has not exceeded 20 active users or 10 GB of stored data. Refunds are processed to the original payment method.

## Invoice Changes
Billing contacts can update invoice details from the billing settings page. Changes made after invoice generation apply to the next billing cycle.

## Failed Payments
If payment fails, the workspace enters a 7-day grace period. Admins receive email notifications on day 1, day 4, and day 7. After the grace period, write access is suspended until payment is resolved.
