# Compliance Vendor Risk

## Vendor Review
All new vendors that process customer data require security review before contract signature. The review includes SOC 2 report inspection, data processing agreement review, and subprocessor analysis.

## Renewal Review
High-risk vendors are reviewed annually. Medium-risk vendors are reviewed every 2 years. Low-risk vendors are reviewed when contract terms or data access materially change.

## Approval
Procurement owns commercial approval. Security owns risk approval. Legal owns contract approval. A vendor cannot be onboarded until all required approvals are complete.
