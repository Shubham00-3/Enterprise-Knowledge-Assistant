# Customer FAQ Support

## Support Tiers
Standard support responds within 2 business days. Business support responds within 8 business hours. Enterprise support provides a 2-hour first response for severity 1 incidents.

## Escalation
Severity 1 incidents include complete service outage, data corruption, or a security incident affecting production data. Escalations should include workspace ID, impact summary, start time, and recent changes.

## Support Channels
Customers can open tickets from the support portal or email support. Enterprise customers may also use their dedicated Slack Connect channel for urgent production issues.
