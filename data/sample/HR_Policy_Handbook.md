# HR Policy Handbook

## Paid Leave
Employees are eligible for 24 paid leaves per calendar year after completing probation. Unused paid leave can carry forward up to 10 days into the next year. Leave requests should be submitted at least 5 business days in advance for planned absences.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave can be taken without advance notice, but employees must notify their manager before the start of the workday when possible. Medical documentation is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive workdays.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Teams handling regulated customer data must follow the secure remote access policy and avoid downloading restricted files to personal devices.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave must be coordinated with HR at least 30 days before the expected start date when possible.
