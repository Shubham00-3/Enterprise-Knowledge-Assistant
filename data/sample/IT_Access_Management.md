# IT Access Management

## Account Lifecycle
Employee accounts are created after HR marks onboarding complete. Accounts are disabled within 4 hours of termination notice and deleted after 90 days unless legal hold applies.

## MFA
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all employees, contractors, and service accounts with console access. Hardware security keys are required for production administrators.

## Access Review
Managers review team access quarterly. System owners review privileged access monthly. Exceptions require documented business justification and expiration date.
