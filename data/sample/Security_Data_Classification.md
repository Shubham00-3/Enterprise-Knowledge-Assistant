# Security Data Classification

## Data Classes
Public data may be shared externally. Internal data is limited to employees and approved contractors. Confidential data includes customer records, financial reports, and product roadmap details. Restricted data includes secrets, credentials, regulated personal data, and incident evidence.

## Handling Rules
Restricted data must be encrypted in transit and at rest. It cannot be copied to personal storage, pasted into unmanaged AI tools, or shared through unapproved channels.

## Retention
Confidential project documents are retained for 3 years unless a legal hold applies. Restricted incident evidence is retained for 7 years and access is reviewed quarterly.
