# Engineering Incident Response

## Incident Declaration
An incident should be declared when customer-facing availability, data integrity, security, or compliance commitments may be affected. The incident commander opens the incident channel and assigns operations, communications, and scribe roles.

## Severity Levels
SEV1 means broad production outage or confirmed data exposure. SEV2 means major feature degradation with workaround. SEV3 means limited impact or single-customer issue.

## Postmortem
SEV1 and SEV2 incidents require a postmortem within 5 business days. The postmortem must include timeline, root cause, customer impact, corrective actions, and owners.
